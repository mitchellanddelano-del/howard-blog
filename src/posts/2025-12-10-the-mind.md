Beloved family, friends, and neighbors, 

I wish you, each of you, an open mind and a willing heart.

I wish you that and an abundance of all of those things that encourage your soul to sing.

For me, the perfect time for quiet contemplation.

And, that's thirsty work😅During the assessment and repair of my heart, soul, and mind I weathered the storms of sadness, I encountered, as well, the sunshine and blue sky  of happiness. And, a million things, if one, in between.

I like to think and believe that I am mindful of how I live my life every day, but this particular intentional examination of where I am in my journey has come to a place of pausing.

I'll return to my post on the morrow, secure in my understanding of who I am and what I am about; a little wiser, a little stronger, and a whole bunch more determined😊.

My intention is to be "better." 

I want to inspire, yes, in the tiniest😊 ways possible, happiness, hope, and laughter, in the hearts and minds of my family, friends, and neighbors; strangers even.

And, I want to thank each one of you for the LARGE inspiration that you have provided for my "life and ways."
