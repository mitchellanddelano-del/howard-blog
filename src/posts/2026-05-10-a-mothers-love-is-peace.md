"A mother's love is peace. It need not be acquired, it need not be deserved."

Erich Fromm



Good Sunday morning,

And Happy Mother's Day, to all of the "heart good mothers," who on the "wings of wonderful" gave and continue to give from "heaven or here," a "love impeccable," a "love forever." 



On this day, no different than any other, I remember my mother, the incomparable, earth-angel, Joyce, who before her time with her three children, and on this earth, was so rudely interrupted by what I considered, as a child, to be one of those mistakes that "God is not supposed to make," to be "perfection realized."

I had this view and opinion as a very young child. Not much in my "child's heart perserved" has changed wherein is considered my opinion, even today.



The cool thing, in my considerable imagination, is that I believe that  all loved children, with a heart of their own, feel nothing less than the massive love for their mothers that I feel for mine.

And that is our bond.



A mother's love, is that universal encouragement that gives power to the hope that we need in this country and the world beyond to move forward, to reach higher, and to live BETTER.

So, what say you we celebrate that reality, and those mothers who deserve no less, on this day and forevermore, than the best of every good and great thing under heaven, yeah?

Say it with me: "Hallelujah!"

Much love and three wheelbarrows of blessings (it's the child's heart in me🤣).

Your friend, Joyce's boy, aka "Fast Binky"🤣
