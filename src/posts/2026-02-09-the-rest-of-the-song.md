Beloved family, friends, and neighbors,

I wish you your BEST ... moment, with every good and great thing possible affixed to it.

Today, inspired by a conversation that I had with the wonderful gentleman who delivers my oxygen on a weekly basis, concerning his heavy heart over losing his furry friend and companion, I dedicate the gateway words/song and the blog following to ALL of the beloved people and pets who have passed on from sharing air, space, and time with us😪 and crossed the rainbow bridge to a place far away, "star far away," but not too far away😊.

My mother, your wife, their friend ... his dog, gone but NEVER forgotten, and ALWAYS available. Just look up into the heavens that you might be reminded to check your heart. They are right THERE and sooo they are ALWAYS right HERE.

From your Freddy to her Fido, all still here, and close enough for you to touch.

Love remembered🤔 can sometimes be grief surrendered😊, yeah?

I woke up this morning sick and unsettled for the recent racism demonstrated by tiny trump and I didn't even want to get out of bed.

Straight truth.

But then I thought about ALL of the good people and things in my life and I remembered as well all of those wonderful people and pets , once in my life, and now in my heart; moment won, day saved.

Let us continue to stand strong and continue to buttress hope in the names of those living in our lives today, and those living in our hearts, forever and forever.

There is nothing else to do: love in the morning, love in the afternoon, love in the evening, and love all through the night. 

Let us NEVER cave to the anger that we righteosly feel concerning EVIL.For to do so only makes us "look like them," in our mirror and to one another in our day.

I look back over the hills and valleys of my life already lived and I wish that I had done better.

Well, now, I can, do better; we ALL can.

L-O-V-E, love.

Ya just gotta know that I care about you, you, and you ... because you matter more than you can possibly know, much more than you can possibly know.

It is on the wings of my caring about you and your mattering to me that I wish you, one and all,

Love, Peace, Joy, and Blessings abundant.

Your friend, Joyce's boy, aka "Fast Binky."🤣
